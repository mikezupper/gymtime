FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate are the forecasts?

Every forecast ships with its own scorecard. Across customers, our 30-day revenue projections land within 4.2% median absolute error — and Meridian shows you its historical accuracy per metric, so you always know how much to trust it.

Does my data leave our warehouse?

No. Meridian pushes computation down to Snowflake, BigQuery, or Postgres and reads only aggregates. Raw rows stay where they live, governed by your existing access controls.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect a warehouse and see their first forecasts in under an hour. Metric definitions import from dbt automatically; the model calibrates on your history overnight.

What counts as a "builder"?

Anyone who creates or edits models, scenarios, or metric definitions. Viewers — people who read dashboards, briefs, and alerts — are free and unlimited on every plan.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month, exports are one click, and your metric definitions are plain YAML in your own repo. No lock-in — we would rather keep you with good forecasts.
