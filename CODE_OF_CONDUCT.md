# Code of Conduct

Gymtime welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience, and perspectives. Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Discuss ideas and code directly without attacking people. Give useful feedback, accept corrections, and leave room for newcomers to ask questions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

The maintainer, [Mike Zupper](https://github.com/mikezupper), may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts when needed. Decisions should reflect the behavior, its impact, and whether it continues after a correction.

For a concern that can be discussed publicly, contact the maintainer in a project issue without repeating harmful material or personal details. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact channel first and share details only after one is agreed. Do not publish someone else's contact information or use a public issue to disclose a private incident.

This policy applies to project spaces and to people representing Gymtime. It does not promise confidentiality for information posted publicly.
